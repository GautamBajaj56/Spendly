Privacy Policy

Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account and log your expenses. This includes your email address, password (encrypted), and the financial transaction data you enter into the application.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the expense tracking service. We use your information to maintain your account, display your spending patterns, and provide you with the financial insights you seek through the app.

Data Storage

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your information is stored securely, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to any third parties.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not currently share your data with third-party services. If we introduce any third-party integrations in the future, we will update this policy and notify you accordingly.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app.
